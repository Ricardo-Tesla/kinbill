Kin-bill Sandbox demo

Voice payments with a family safety check

Your family has your back before money moves.

Grace, 74, pays people by speaking. An AI scam check and her own rules decide: safe payments go straight through PayPal, risky ones wait for her son David.

G I want to pay someoneGrace's screen: speak, check, send → D I'm the helperDavid's screen: review held payments →

How it works

  1. Grace says it"Pay my plumber Joe 80 dollars." Kin-bill reads it back and she answers yes or no.
  2. AI checks, her rules decideAn AI looks for scam signs. Plain rules she set with David decide. The AI never moves money.
  3. Paid, or held for DavidSafe: paid through PayPal at once. Risky: held until David approves. No answer in time: cancelled, nothing moves.

PayPal sandbox demo: no real money is used. Kin-bill is an independent project built on PayPal's APIs for the PayPal "Build What's Next" hackathon. It is not made or endorsed by PayPal.
